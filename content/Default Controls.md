- WASD to move
- Spacebar to jump
- Move your mouse to look around
- Q to grab with your left hand
- E to grab with your right hand
- Alt to switch between "toggle" and "hold" grab modes
- Scroll wheel to adjust how far away you hold your grabbed object
- P to uncapture your mouse (for debugging purposes)
